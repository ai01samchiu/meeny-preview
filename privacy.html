MEENY MINY MOE 私隱政策

MEENY MINY MOE 係一人美甲工作室自用嘅私人作品紀錄 App，只供工作室東主本人使用，唔開放公眾註冊。

  • App 只會透過 Google Drive（drive.file 權限）讀寫由 App 自己建立嘅檔案，即作品相片同備份，唔會讀取 Drive 其他檔案。
  • 相片儲存喺用戶自己嘅 Google Drive；文字資料儲存喺 Supabase 私人資料庫，只有用戶本人可以存取。
  • 資料唔會出售、分享或用作廣告。
  • 用戶可隨時喺 Google 帳戶設定移除授權，並可自行刪除 Drive 入面嘅檔案。

Privacy Policy (English)

MEENY MINY MOE is a private portfolio app for a single nail studio owner; public sign-up is closed. It uses the Google Drive drive.file scope only to create and read files the app itself made (work photos and backups). Text data is stored in a private Supabase database accessible only to the owner. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account settings.
